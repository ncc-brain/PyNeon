Project Governance
==================

The PyNeon project is currently maintained by:

- Qian Chu (https://github.com/qian-chu)
- Jan-Gabriel Hartel (https://github.com/JGHartel)

We strive to respond to issues and feature requests in a timely manner, alongside our regular research responsibilities.

While the maintainers guide the overall direction of the project, community input is highly valued. Experienced users and contributors are warmly invited to get involved and, over time, to join the maintainer team.
